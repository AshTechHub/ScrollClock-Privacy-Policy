# ScrollClock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ScrollClock ("the app") is an Android app published by AshMade ("I", "me"). It counts the Instagram Reels you scroll through each day and shows you the number, together with the time you spend on Reels. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Contact: appsbyashtech@gmail.com

## The short version

- Your reel counts, time spent, settings and mascot picture stay on your phone. We run no servers for the app and cannot see any of it.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services, apart from Google Play's own billing library, which handles the optional one-time purchase.
- The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else.
- The only time the app contacts the internet is through Google Play's billing library for the optional purchase. It talks to Google Play, not to us. This is why the app lists the internet and network-status permissions. The app also lists the vibration permission, used only for the optional milestone vibration.
- The app can make your phone vibrate briefly when a milestone message appears. This uses Android’s vibration permission, which gives the app no access to your information. You can turn it off in the app.
- The app is excluded from Android's backup.
- You can erase your counts inside the app at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling.

## How the app uses Android's Accessibility service

ScrollClock uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose only: to notice when you swipe to the next reel in Instagram, so it can count it, measure how long the Reels screen is open, and show the floating counter. It is not used for anything else, and the app never performs actions on your behalf.

While Instagram is open, Android tells the app:

- which screen is showing,
- the technical names and positions of the on-screen elements that are visible, and
- when the Reels feed scrolls.

The app does not read the text on your screen. It does not read your messages, comments, posts, usernames or passwords, and it does not take screenshots or record your screen. Android's Accessibility permission is broad and would technically allow an app to do those things, but ScrollClock does not use it that way. Android also reports when you open other apps. The app ignores those events, except to hide the floating counter when you leave Instagram.

The Accessibility service is off until you switch it on yourself in Android settings. Before that, the app shows a notice that explains the points above, and you must tap "Agree and continue". If you tap "Deny", nothing is turned on. You can switch the service off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings. If you do, the app asks for your agreement again the next time you enable the counter.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your phone, in its private storage. We cannot see it.

- **Days the counter was running**: a list of dates (no times), used to work out your streaks of days with few reels.
- **Your daily reel counts**: a date and a number.
- **Time spent on Reels**: how long the Reels screen was open each day. If you stop swiping for more than three minutes, the extra time is not counted.
- **Troubleshooting information**: a few timestamps (such as when the service started, when Instagram was last seen, when the Reels screen was last recognised and when the last reel was counted). If the app cannot recognise the Reels screen, it may also keep a short list of the technical names of reel-related screen elements it noticed and the Instagram version that was installed when the Reels screen was last recognised. This powers the Status and "Reel Counter Disappeared?" help in the Help tab.
- **Settings and small bits of state**: for example whether milestone messages are on, whether vibration on milestones is on, and the date, count and wording of the last milestone message shown (so the same one is not repeated straight away), your chosen pill color.
- **Your agreement** to the Accessibility notice.
- **Whether the mascot unlock is active**: a simple yes or no, so the app does not have to wait for the store each time.
- **A custom mascot picture, if you choose one**: the picture is copied into the app's private storage, made smaller, and any editing (such as removing the background) happens on your phone. The app can only read the single picture or file you pick in Android's photo picker, and the picture never leaves your phone.

The messages the app shows (milestone banners on the floating counter, the short line under your time spent, and the streak messages on the Stats page) are chosen from text built into the app, based only on your counts, time and streaks. No information about you leaves your phone to produce them. When a milestone message appears, the app can also vibrate your phone briefly. Whether it vibrates, and how many taps, depends only on your count. No information leaves your phone to do this.

## The optional mascot purchase (Google Play)

Using your own mascot picture is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play.

- Google Play handles the payment. Your card or payment details never reach us, and Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase.
- To do this, the app uses Google Play's billing library, which needs an internet connection. The app uses it to show the price, start the purchase, and check what your Google account has bought. It checks again when the app opens, so a refund removes the unlock. It also does this after you reinstall the app, or when you tap "Restore purchase".
- We have no accounts. Your purchase is tied to your Google account in Google Play, not to us.
- Google may give us limited order information in Play Console (for example the order number, the product, the date and the country). We use it only to handle purchases, refunds and support.

## Information we receive

We receive nothing automatically. The only exception is the optional "Email report" button in the Help tab. If you tap it, the app opens your own email app with a report already written. The report starts with a space where you can describe the problem, then an automatic part containing your phone’s manufacturer and model, your Android version (API level), the ScrollClock version, your Instagram version (the app checks whether Instagram is installed, and which version, only for this report), whether the service is on, the timing information described above, and the technical names of Instagram screen elements. The automatic part does not include your reel counts. What you type in the description is up to you, so please do not include personal information. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app. You can read and edit the whole report first.

If you send it, we receive the report and your email address, plus anything else you write. We use them only to look into counting problems and to reply to you, and we keep them for up to 12 months, or delete them sooner on request.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. The app contains no advertising or analytics code and no third-party SDKs other than Google Play's billing library described above. If you email us a report, it passes through your email provider and ours, as any email does.

## Backup and reinstalling

ScrollClock is excluded from Android's backup. If you reinstall the app or set up a new phone, your counts, settings and mascot picture will not be restored, and the Accessibility notice will be shown again. If you bought the mascot unlock, it comes back automatically when the app checks with Google Play, or when you tap "Restore purchase" in the Premium tab.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- Switch the Accessibility service off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.
- Tap "Reset all counts" in the Help tab to erase your saved counts, time-spent and streak history.
- Tap "Use default" in the Premium tab to remove your custom mascot picture.
- Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.
- Because we hold no personal data from the app itself, there is nothing for us to export or delete from it. If you have emailed us and want a copy of what you sent, or want it deleted, write to appsbyashtech@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights over your personal information, and we will honor them for any information we hold.
- Your purchase record is held by Google Play. You can manage it through your Google account.
- Turn off “Vibrate on milestones” (or “Milestone messages”) in the Stats tab under Settings.

## Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android phone. Because the app does not send your data anywhere, there is no server for us to protect.
